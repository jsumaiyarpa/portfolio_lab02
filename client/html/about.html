About Portfolio Generator

Portfolio Generator is a simple web application that helps users create, manage, and share their personal portfolios easily.

What You Can Do

  • Add your personal information
  • Add education and experience
  • Add skills and projects
  • Choose a portfolio template
  • Generate and share your portfolio

One Shareable Link

Once your portfolio is generated, you can share the public portfolio link with others. If you later edit your portfolio, the same public link automatically reflects the latest changes when the link is refreshed. This means you do not need to create or share a new link every time you update your portfolio.
